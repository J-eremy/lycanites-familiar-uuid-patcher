LYCANITES MOBS - FAMILIAR UUID PATCHER
======================================

1. Copy your lycanitesmobs jar into the "put-jar-here" folder.

2. Run the patcher:
      Windows:  double-click Patch-Windows.bat
      Linux:    open a terminal here and run:  bash Patch-Linux.sh

3. Press Enter when it asks which UUID to use.

4. Copy BOTH files from the "patched" folder into your Minecraft
   "mods" folder, after deleting the old lycanitesmobs jar there.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see README.md

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your original jar is never modified.
