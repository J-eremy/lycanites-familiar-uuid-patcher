PUT YOUR LYCANITES MOBS JAR IN THIS FOLDER
==========================================

Copy the Lycanites Mobs jar out of your mods folder and put it here.

It is the file called something like:

    lycanitesmobs-1.12.2-2.0.8.10.jar

Where is my mods folder?
  - Official Minecraft launcher:  %appdata%\.minecraft\mods
  - CurseForge / Prism / MultiMC / ATLauncher:  the instance's "mods" folder

Put ONLY that one jar in here. Keep the original where it is until the patcher
has finished, so you still have a working game if anything goes wrong.

Then run Patch-Windows.bat (Windows) or Patch-Linux.sh (Linux).
